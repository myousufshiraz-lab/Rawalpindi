Rawalpindi is the fourth-most populous city in Pakistan, located in the northern Punjab province along the Pothohar Plateau. Often called "Pindi," it sits adjacent to the national capital, Islamabad, forming the core of the shared "twin cities" metropolitan region. The city serves as a major military stronghold, housing the General Headquarters (GHQ) of the Pakistan Army. Known for its lively historic commercial hubs like Raja Bazar, it blends rich colonial-era architecture with a fast-paced urban atmosphere.
